# Analysis charter

## Learning objective

Explore a rental dataset, visualize variable relationships, and practice fitting/interpreting a simple ordinary least squares model with Statsmodels.

## Scope represented today

- Load and inspect a local CSV dataset.
- Explore summary statistics, distributions, and correlations.
- Plot the relationship between area and rental value.
- Fit and interpret a simple OLS regression.

## Not in scope

- A deployable price-estimation product or automated recommendation.
- Data collection, data-source licensing, consumer privacy review, or geographic-market validation.
- Causal inference, fairness analysis, robust feature engineering, model monitoring, or financial decisioning.
- Reproducible packaging, CI, test automation, or production operations.

## Stakeholder and decision gate

This is a personal learning archive. Any reuse of the data or conversion of the notebook into a model requires owner approval plus a verified data source, usage rights, and a separately scoped analytics charter.

## Criteria for a future analytical product

1. Record dataset provenance, license, purpose, quality checks, privacy classification, and retention policy.
2. Create a reproducible environment and separate raw, processed, and derived data artifacts.
3. Define prediction target, users, error tolerance, and prohibited decision uses.
4. Evaluate baselines and holdout/cross-validation performance; test regression assumptions and subgroup effects.
5. Establish review, monitoring, documentation, and accountable ownership before deployment.
